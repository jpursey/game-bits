# The Passes section, drawn over the grid while shown: a world pass of turning
# quads with water over it in the frame, and the depth of the world pass and of
# a depth-only pass beside it. The world pass is multisampled, so the quads'
# edges are smooth in its color, though not in its depth.
wait 10
key s

# The sections above are collapsed to bring this one into the window.
click "Frames"
click "Instances"
click "Textures"
click "Texture arrays"
click "GUI"
click "Blend modes"
click "Texture formats"
click "Volume textures"
click "Storage buffers"
click "Unset bindings"
click "Mesh pools"
wait 20
save passes

# The quads have turned, and the waves have moved.
wait 30
save passes_later

# Without multisampling, the quads' edges in the world pass's color are jagged.
click "Multisample the world pass"
wait 2
save passes_single

# Multisampled again.
click "Multisample the world pass"
wait 2
save passes_multisampled

# In a smaller window, the targets that follow the frame are smaller. The
# world's depth is still the quads', at a size where NVIDIA's driver needs the
# multisampled depth stored to resolve it (see VulkanPass).
run resize_window 1085 399
wait 10
save passes_resized
run resize_window 1280 800
wait 10

# Hidden again, the grid is back.
key s
wait 2
save hidden
